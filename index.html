Frequently asked questions

How do I clock in?
Open the app and tap the clock-in button on the main screen. Syslo starts recording your time immediately. Tap Clock Out when you’re done. If you forgot to clock in or out on time, you can adjust the start or finish time before confirming.
How do I create a new timesheet?
Go to the Sheets tab and tap the + button. Give your sheet a name — a client name, job title, or pay period — and it’s ready to use.
Can I edit a time entry after the fact?
Yes. Open the relevant sheet, tap the entry you want to change, and you can update the start time, end time, or any notes.
What happens if I forget to clock out?
When you tap Clock Out, Syslo lets you pick the time you actually finished. You can also open the sheet afterwards and edit the entry.
How do I set up overtime rules?
When creating or editing a sheet, expand the Advanced section. You can set an overtime threshold (hours per day), an OT pay multiplier, a red day multiplier for public holidays, and choose whether to round hours.
How do I export a PDF report?
Open a sheet, tap the export button, and choose PDF. A clean, professional report will be generated and ready to save or share in a few taps.
Can I sync my data with iCloud?
Yes. Go to Settings → Storage and turn on Sync with iCloud. Your sheets and entries are then kept in your own iCloud account and synced between your iPhone and iPad. Sync is off by default — without it, everything stays on your device only.
Can I show hours as 7t45m instead of 7,75t?
Yes. In Settings → Hour Display you can choose between decimal hours (7.75 h) and hours and minutes (7 h 45 m). The choice applies across the app and in exported reports.
How do I move an entry from Unassigned to a sheet?
Open Unassigned in the Sheets tab and tap the arrow on the entry. Choose any active sheet — or a sheet that has ended but not been exported yet — and the entry is moved.
Does Syslo require an account or subscription?
No account needed and no subscription. Syslo is a one-time download with no ongoing fees.
How do I delete a sheet or an entry?
Swipe left on any entry or sheet to reveal the delete option. This action cannot be undone, so double-check before confirming.
What iOS version does Syslo require?
Syslo requires iOS 17.0 or later and works on both iPhone and iPad.

Privacy

Syslo does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers — there are none. All data you enter — clock-in times, sheet names, and notes — stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is stored in your own private iCloud account, handled by Apple. We have no access to it either way.
